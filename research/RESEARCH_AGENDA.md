# What evidence should come next?

The completed comparison finds smoother ridge exposures and a small, uncertain
reconstruction improvement. The next work should explain that uncertainty rather
than expand a leaderboard of penalties and windows.

| Unrun experiment | Missing input or design | Claim it could resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Later-period replication with the same specification | A genuinely later complete factor release and frozen estimator settings | Whether the 1.93% point-estimate MSE reduction persists |
| Independent return reconciliation | Authorized issuer NAV total-return or second-vendor series with distribution conventions | Whether adjusted-price revisions materially affect the attribution |
| Separate mandate changes from statistical noise | Dated holdings, benchmark constituent histories and documented transition dates | Whether beta drift reflects holdings changes rather than weak estimation |
| Release-aware operational attribution | Archived factor publication timestamps and source vintages for each decision date | What exposure estimates could actually have been produced then |
| Specialist industry extension | Fixed extra ETF families and a revised endpoint that acknowledges overlapping holdings | Whether industry concentration changes the bias–variance trade-off |
| Targeted residual-risk model | Prespecified economic exposures, such as rates or energy, with clear timing and a later validation sample | Whether unexplained residual means reflect omitted risk rather than alpha |
| Implementable hedge study | Tradable hedge instruments, financing, turnover, fills and transaction costs | Whether a factor attribution can support a useful hedging decision |

None of these experiments is claimed as completed. In particular, the legacy IGN fund
cannot simply be relabeled IDGT: the December 2023 change altered its mandate as well
as its ticker. A holdings-aware extension must treat that change explicitly.

Stronger shrinkage and lower rank already have unfavorable outcomes in the bounded
sensitivity table. That is evidence against choosing complexity or dimensionality
reduction by presentation appeal. The original 60-month result remains the anchor.
